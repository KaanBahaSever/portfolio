title: Launched Açık Matematik
date: 2026-06-02
dateLabel: June 2026
---

Started [Açık Matematik](/projects/acik-matematik/), an open-source academic publishing
platform and modern textbook initiative for undergraduate mathematics in Turkish, built on
reproducible numerical computing and rigorous documentation. It is published at
[acik-matematik.com](https://acik-matematik.com).
