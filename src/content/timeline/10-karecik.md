Started building [Karecik](/projects/karecik/), a multi-tenant QR menu platform that local
cafés and restaurants use for customer interaction and menu and order management. It is
written in Go on PostgreSQL and Cloudflare, engineered for zero downtime and low latency, and
open source under the GPL-3.0.
