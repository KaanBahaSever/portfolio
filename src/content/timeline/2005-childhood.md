From a young age I wanted to know how things worked. I took apart the devices at home and put
them back together, and I tinkered with electrical and electronic parts; even the power sockets
did not escape my curiosity.

I did not have a computer of my own until 2005. Before that I grew up tinkering with my
cousins’ computers, and it was on them that I explored the system and the games.

Over time that curiosity about hardware and about exploring things grew into a love of systems
programming and software development. It is why most of what I build today still sits close to
the machine.
