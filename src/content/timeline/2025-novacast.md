Built [Novacast](/projects/novacast/) at a computer company where I worked, to replace software
that was bloated and fell short of our needs. It manages many screens of different makes from
one web panel, on Windows, macOS, Linux, Samsung TV, LG TV and Android TV. A small Rust player
runs on each screen, and a Go server reaches the screens through an MQTT broker. It is in
production.
