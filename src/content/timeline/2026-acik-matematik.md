[Açık Matematik](/projects/acik-matematik/) went live at
[acik-matematik.com](https://acik-matematik.com): an open-source academic publishing platform
and modern textbook initiative for undergraduate mathematics in Turkish, built on reproducible
numerical computing and rigorous documentation. I conceived it in 2023 to fix my own friction
with academic lecture notes, stepped up development in March 2026, and announced the
open-source release in September 2026.
