Shipped [Karecik](/projects/karecik/) as a production product: a multi-tenant QR menu platform
that local cafés and restaurants use. It started with a QR menu I made for a middle-school
friend’s café, and that friend now uses it in all three of his branches. The first prototypes
date from 2021 under earlier working titles. It is written in Go on PostgreSQL, built for zero
downtime and low latency, and open source under the GPL-3.0.
